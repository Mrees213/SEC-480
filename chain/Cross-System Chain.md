# Cross-System Chain Prediction

For Project 1, I will use Google Drive as the source system and GitHub as the destination system, with Claude acting as the system that moves the information between them.

I predict that the most fragile part of the chain will be the handoff from the source content into GitHub.

I expect the information to successfully cross between the systems, but I also expect that the destination may contain additional formatting or automatically generated text that was not present in the original source.

This prediction is based on the idea that a successful action in each individual system does not necessarily prove that the information arrived at the destination exactly as intended.

The chain will be considered successful only after I verify the final result directly in GitHub.
