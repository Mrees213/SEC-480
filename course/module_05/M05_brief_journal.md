**Where:** your `sec-480` repository, at `journal/module-05.md`

**Length:** short. A few paragraphs, not an essay.

## What is being graded

Not whether you were right. Whether there is an entry, and whether each answer engages with the
question that was actually asked.

An answer that is two words, or the question pasted back, or an answer to a different question,
gets partial credit. A missing heading gets none. An honest answer saying a thing did not work
and why gets full credit.

That is the whole standard. It is deliberately cheap to satisfy and impossible to fake.

**You write this entry yourself.** An agent can help with the lab; it does not write the journal. An entry an agent wrote earns partial credit on every heading.

## This module's entry

Six headings, a few sentences each. **An act is one of the numbered sections of the lab**, and
the labels below point back at those sections in `M05_lab_nobody-is-watching.md`.

1. **The ticket I could not honestly answer.** Act 1. Which one, and what it was missing. If you
   answered it anyway on the first pass, say that. Most people do and it is the most useful thing
   in this entry.

2. **What the queue looked like as a queue.** Act 1. Six tickets by six people. Did working them
   in order tell you anything that working one would not have?

3. **Day one against day three.** Act 2. You read the first recap properly. Be honest about the
   second and third. What does that suggest about a report nobody is obliged to read?

4. **My prediction, and what actually happened.** Act 3. Did it match? If you expected an error
   and got a clean report, write what that felt like to read.

5. **What would have told me, and what would not.** Act 3. Looking at the output of a procedure
   you know was broken: what in it gives the break away, and what looks exactly like success?

6. **Teaching something to stop.** Act 4. Writing down what your procedure refuses to do is a
   different kind of work from writing down what it does. Was it harder? What did you find you
   had never decided?

## What a good entry is not

Not a summary of the lab. Not a list of commands. Not a status report saying the work is
complete.

The test is whether someone reading it learns something they could not get from your files.
