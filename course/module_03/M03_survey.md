# M03 Survey: your starting point

**Not graded. Not evaluated for correctness. There is no answer here that counts against you.**

This is the first of these you will complete, and it is your baseline. Every later one gets compared
against your own answers here rather than against anyone else's, so the only thing any of it can
measure is your own movement.

Where you start is not the point. Movement is the point, and an honest low rating today is worth
more to you than a generous one, because a generous baseline hides the progress you actually
make.

## How to complete it

Copy this file into your repository as `surveys/module-03.md` and fill it in there. Due with the
rest of the module, **Thursday 17 September, 9:00 pm**.

Complete it **after you finish module 3**, not before. Everything below asks about module 3 itself.

Answer in one sitting, and do not research anything before rating yourself. The rating is about
what you can do right now without looking it up.

Every question is either a number from 1 to 5 or a pick from a list. Nothing here needs a paragraph.

## The scale

**1 is low, 5 is high.** This measures **efficacy**, what you can
actually accomplish, not how efficient you are at it. Slow and correct is a high rating.

**Rating** | **Means**
-----------|----------
1 | I have not worked with this
2 | I have followed instructions in it, but could not do it unaided
3 | I can do routine work in it unaided, and I know when I am stuck
4 | I can handle unfamiliar problems in it and usually work them out
5 | I could teach it, and I know where my own knowledge runs out
