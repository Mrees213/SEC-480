**Not graded. Not evaluated for correctness. There is no answer here that counts against you.**

## The scale

**1 is low, 5 is high.** This measures **efficacy**, what you can actually accomplish, not how
efficient you are at it. Slow and correct is a high rating.

**Rating** | **Means**
-----------|----------
1 | I have not worked with this
2 | I have followed instructions in it, but could not do it unaided
3 | I can do routine work in it unaided, and I know when I am stuck
4 | I can handle unfamiliar problems in it and usually work them out
5 | I could teach it, and I know where my own knowledge runs out
